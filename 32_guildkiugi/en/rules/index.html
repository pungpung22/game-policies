Rules

Effective: November 19, 2026 (first edition)

These Rules set out the community and prohibited conduct sections of the Terms of Service in detail. You must agree to these Rules before you use chat.

1. Scope

These Rules apply to guild leader names, Intros, chat (All, Alliance and 1:1), friend requests, Alliance names and Alliance descriptions, and every place where you play with other players, such as rankings, the Arena, real-time battles and the Boss Hunt.

2. Eligibility

  • Features for competing or talking with other players are available only to players who link a Google or Apple account. Guests can only view rankings and chat, but they can report and block.
  • Chat and 1:1 messages are available after you confirm you are 14 or older and agree to these Rules.
  • 1:1 messages are only between friends. There are limits on the number of friends and on the number of friend requests you can send per day, and received friend requests expire after 7 days.

3. What not to do

The following are prohibited everywhere, in names, Intros, messages, Alliance names and Alliance descriptions.

  • Swearing, harassment or bullying, and insults aimed at other players
  • Hate or discrimination (race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, disability, age and so on)
  • Sexual content, and content that sexualizes minors
  • Threats, or words that encourage violence
  • Spam, advertising or promotion, and links that lead to other services
  • Exposing personal information, yours or anyone else's (real name, address, phone number, email, messenger or social media accounts, etc.)
  • Impersonating staff or other players, and scams
  • Buying or selling accounts, game currencies or items for cash, and sharing accounts
  • Modified apps or automation programs, knowingly exploiting bugs, and deliberately disconnecting from real-time battles to change the result
  • Repeatedly making false reports

4. Names and Intros

  • Guild leader names use letters and numbers within the set character limit, and you cannot use a name someone else is already using. The character limit and the date when you can change your name again for free are shown in the Change Name window; before that date, you can change it right away using Gems. Guests can change their name after linking an account.
  • An Intro is a short single line, and it is not saved if it contains banned words.
  • Names that do not follow these Rules are changed to a default name, and you can then choose a new name right away. Intros that do not follow these Rules are deleted, and you may be unable to write one again for a while.

5. Reporting and blocking

  • You can report messages (All, Alliance and 1:1), profiles (name and Intro) and Alliances (name and description). Choose one reason: swearing or harassment, hate or discrimination, sexual content, threats, spam or advertising, exposing personal information, or other.
  • When you report, a copy of the reported message, or of the name, Intro, Alliance name or description at that time, is sent to our operations team, and the reported message is hidden on your screen right away. It is removed for other players only after the operations team reviews it.
  • You can report the same message or target only once, and there is a limit on the number of reports you can send per day.
  • When you block someone, their All and Alliance chat messages and their summon announcement lines no longer appear in your chat, you are no longer friends, and you no longer receive their friend requests, 1:1 messages or friendly match requests. They still appear in rankings, Alliance member lists and as Arena opponents. You can unblock them in Chat > Blocked.
  • Reporting and blocking are separate. Reporting someone does not block them.

6. Actions we take

Depending on how serious the violation is and whether it is repeated, we take the following actions.

  • Hiding or deleting messages
  • Muting (you cannot use chat or 1:1 messages for a set time; the remaining time is shown in the input box)
  • Resetting names, Intros, Alliance names or Alliance descriptions
  • Restricting friends and 1:1 messages, and excluding you from rankings, real-time battles and the Boss Hunt
  • Suspending the account (for serious violations such as modified apps or payment abuse)

If an action is taken against you, we tell you the reason and the duration in the Game.

7. Appeals

If you disagree with an action, let us know at pungpungstudi@hello-support.com with your support code (Settings > Account · Log out · Delete account > Copy Support Code). Tapping [Appeal] in the suspension notice opens an email that already contains the information we need. We will review the action again and tell you the result by email.

8. Record retention

To handle reports, we keep real-time chat evidence for 14 days, 1:1 and Alliance messages for 180 days, and handled reports for 365 days after handling. Unhandled reports are kept until they are handled. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
