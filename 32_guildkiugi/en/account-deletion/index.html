Account Deletion

Effective: November 19, 2026 (first edition)

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Plush Knights (developer: PUNGPUNGSTUDI). Guest accounts can be deleted too.

Deleting in the Game

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Account · Log out · Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete Account, check what will be deleted and what will remain, then tap Delete once more.

Only after the game server successfully deletes your account does the Game delete the save data, login information and terms consent records on your device and return to the first screen. If deletion fails, it is not shown as complete, and you can try again. For an account that signed in with Apple, the link to the Apple account is also removed.

If you cannot use the app (request by email)

Send an email to pungpungstudi@hello-support.com as follows.

  • Subject: [Malanggom Guild] Account deletion request (the same prefix as the in-game Contact mail)
  • Body: your guild leader name, support code (if you know it), how you signed in (Google, Apple or guest), and information that lets us confirm the account (such as part of the order number of a recent payment)

After verifying your identity, we will delete your account and the data below within 30 days and tell you the result by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account, login links, and cloud saves (progress, bears, gear, pets, etc.)
  • Gems and server currencies, Mail, and ranking and Arena records
  • Public profile, friend and block lists, Alliance membership, notification registration and promo notification consent records
  • Save data, login information and terms consent records on your device (when you delete in the Game)

What remains

  • Payment, subscription and cancellation (withdrawal) records: 5 years as required by law (Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Minimum transaction records, and audit logs with personal details removed, kept so that purchases from a deleted account are not delivered again
  • Chat and 1:1 messages you already sent may remain on the other players' screens, and they are deleted 180 days after the day they were sent.
  • Records of reports still being handled (until they are handled) and server recovery copies (destroyed within 30 days of creation)
  • Information processed by Google, Apple and Firebase is kept under each company's policies.

For detailed retention periods, see section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

Check before you delete

  • This cannot be undone. A deleted account and its progress cannot be restored.
  • Auto-renewing subscriptions (Membership and Plush Knights Season Pass) do not stop when you delete your account. Please cancel them in Google Play or the App Store first.
  • After deletion, you cannot receive the same purchases again on a new account.
