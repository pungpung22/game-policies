Rules

  • Gems and Bear Summon Tickets have the same rates. Your summon level rises with your total summons (gems and tickets both count); if it rises in the middle of a 10-pull, the new level's table applies from the next pull.
  • 10-pull guarantee: In a 10-pull, if the first 9 have no SR or higher and the 10th is also below SR, the 10th becomes SR. All 16 SR bears are equally likely (6.25% each). Single pulls repeated do not get this guarantee.
  • UR+ pity: If 59 pulls pass with no UR or higher, the 60th pull is UR or higher. Its grade follows the ‘grade rates on a pity pull’ table below.
  • LR pity: Once LR is unlocked (counting from summon Lv4), if 199 pulls pass with no LR or higher, the 200th pull is LR. MR pity: Once MR is unlocked (counting from Lv12), if 199 pulls pass with no MR, the 200th pull is MR. If pity triggers overlap on one pull, only one applies, in the order MR, LR, UR+.
  • Rate-up (pickup): UR, LR and MR each have one pickup bear. When that grade comes up, 50% of it is the pickup bear and the rest is shared evenly by the other bears of that grade. If you miss the pickup, the next bear of that grade is guaranteed to be the pickup bear (0% for the others on that pull). A miss carries over when the pickup changes, giving the pickup bear at that time. The current pickup bears and time left are shown on the summon screen and in [Drop Rates] > [Per Bear].
  • Mileage: 1 point per pull (gems and tickets). With 100 points you pick one UR bear, 200 points one LR and 300 points one MR (LR and MR only after that grade is unlocked). You choose, so there are no rates.
  • Pity counts, missed pickups, mileage and summon level carry over when the pickup changes.
