Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 (first edition) · updated the same day: added how to reach Ad privacy choices in Settings

PUNGPUNGSTUDI (the “Developer”) explains below how it handles personal information in the mobile game Plush Knights (Android and iPhone com.pungpungstudi.woopakiugi, the “Game”).

Summary: When you first open the Game, you agree to the terms and then start with Google (Android), Apple (iPhone) or as a guest. The Game does not ask you to enter your name, email or phone number. Your progress is saved on your device first and backed up to the game server (GameStack). Features you share with other players, such as rankings, chat, friends, Alliances and the Arena, are available only if you link a Google or Apple account. The only ads are rewarded ads (Google AdMob) that you choose to watch, and we do not use personalized ads. Analytics and diagnostics (Firebase) are sent only for players 14 or older who gave [Optional] consent when they first opened the Game. Payments are handled by Google Play and the App Store. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Account · Log out · Delete account > Delete Account.

1. Information we process and why

We process the information you need to play the Game in order to perform our agreement with you, and we process [Optional] items only if you consent.

Start-up consent record

  • The version (date) of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy you confirmed, the time you confirmed them, and whether you consented to [Optional] analytics and diagnostics. This is kept only on this device and is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Purpose: confirming consent, and asking again when the terms change

Guest account

  • A random install identifier created by the app (not a unique device number), player ID, guild leader name (a default name at first), and login session token
  • A guest account can only be continued on that device. Before you delete the app or change devices, please link a Google or Apple account in Settings.
  • Purpose: identifying the account, keeping you logged in, and saving and restoring progress

Account linking (optional)

  • On Android you can start with a Google account, and on iPhone with an Apple account, or link one to a guest account. The app sends the tokens needed to verify your login to the game server. For Sign in with Apple, it also sends a one-time authorization code used to remove the link when you delete your account.
  • The app does not ask you to enter your name or email. However, a Google login token may contain your Google account email address, and the server may keep it to verify the linked account. Sign in with Apple does not request your name or email.

Game progress and cloud saves

  • All save data, including guild, bear, gear, pet, building, quest and adventure records, game currencies such as Gold, and game settings
  • Purpose: saving on your device first and then backing up to the server to protect your progress when you reinstall the app or change devices, and preventing abuse

Server currency, purchases, subscriptions, coupons and mail

  • Gem balances (paid and free) and records of Gems granted and used; receipts and grant records for summons and ad rewards
  • Purchase verification data: store name, product ID, transaction ID or purchase token, purchase and refund status, and delivery records
  • Auto-renewing subscriptions (Membership and Plush Knights Season Pass): the subscription product, start, renewal and end times, payment status (active, grace period, on hold, cancelled, refunded), and records of daily mail received and Season Pass rewards claimed
  • Coupons (Android app): the coupon code you entered, the time of use, and the number of wrong entries per day
  • Mail claim records
  • Purpose: verifying payments, delivering products and benefits, preventing duplicate delivery, restoring purchases, checking subscription status, handling refunds, and preventing improper entries. Payment details such as card numbers are processed by Google and Apple; the Game does not receive them.

Rankings, Arena, Alliance and Boss Hunt (if you link an account)

  • Combat power, best field, scores and ranks, Arena defense formation, and Alliance name, description, role and contribution records
  • Real-time battles and friendly matches: match number and seed, both players' input records, the result and the state values used to verify it, and the opponent's player ID
  • When you submit a record, the game server receives the result of a device integrity check (Google Play Integrity, Apple App Attest).
  • Purpose: providing rankings, battles and Alliance features, verifying results and preventing cheating, and granting rewards

Public profile

  • Guild leader name, Intro, the look and outfit of the first bear in your formation, title and frame, guild level, combat power and rank, best field, Alliance name and role, and your deployed bears
  • This information is visible to other players in rankings, chat, Alliances and the Arena.

Chat, friends and 1:1 messages (optional, if you link an account)

  • You can use chat and 1:1 messages only after you confirm you are 14 or older and agree to the Rules. Guests can only view rankings and chat.
  • All, Alliance and 1:1 chat messages, your friend list, friend requests you sent and received, and your block list
  • If you summon a high grade (UR or higher), the system line in chat may show your guild leader name and the bear and grade you summoned.
  • Your name, Intro and messages are visible to the people you talk with, Alliance members, and other players in chat, rankings and the Arena. 1:1 messages are visible only to that friend.
  • Purpose: providing chat, friends, 1:1 messages and Alliance features, filtering banned words, and handling reports and restrictions

Reports (including guests)

  • The player IDs of the reporter and the reported player, the reason, and a copy of the reported message (including 1:1 messages) or of the name, Intro, Alliance name or Alliance description at that time
  • Purpose: reviewing reports and acting on Rules violations (removing messages, muting, resetting names or Intros, suspending use)

Notifications (optional)

  • If you turn on Game notifications, we register a notification token for sending notifications to your device (on Android, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token; on iPhone, a token obtained through the Apple Push Notification service (APNs)) on the game server, together with your player ID and platform. Notifications for full offline rewards and finished construction are scheduled on your device only.
  • Promo notifications (optional, 14 or older): whether you consented, whether you consented to promo notifications at night (21:00–08:00), the date and time of consent or withdrawal, the recipient's time zone, and the app version
  • Purpose: sending notifications, confirming consent to promo notifications, and informing you of your consent status every 2 years

Analytics and diagnostics (optional, 14 or older)

  • Sent only if you consented to [Optional] analytics and diagnostics on the terms consent screen when you first opened the Game, or in Settings. It is off by default.
  • Device type, operating system and app version, app instance identifier, approximate region estimated from your IP address, game progress events and error logs, and player ID. We do not send your name or contact details.
  • Purpose: improving the Game, finding errors, and finding where progress gets stuck

Contact email (optional)

  • If you send an email to our contact address: the sender, recipient and reply-to email addresses, subject, body, attached images, email headers, time received and delivery status, and replies sent by the Developer
  • The guild leader name and support code you give us to verify your identity, and anything else you write in your inquiry (app version and platform, payment time, part of an order number, etc.). The in-game [Contact] puts only the app version, platform, time and support code in the email body.
  • Purpose: answering inquiries, handling rights requests such as access or deletion and account deletion requests, and checking payments and errors

2. Ads and consent management

The Game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch; there are no interstitial or banner ads. We use Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and its ad partners may process the following information to serve ads, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, measure performance and diagnose errors.

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device identifiers such as app-scoped identifiers, device, operating system and app version, and network information
  • Interactions such as ad impressions, completed views and taps, consent status, and diagnostic information

Where required (such as the European Economic Area and the UK), UMP shows a privacy choices screen, and in those regions you can reopen and change it at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices. We do not use personalized ads, and we do not use advertising IDs (Android Advertising ID, iPhone IDFA). We do not request Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. The Developer does not send player IDs to AdMob. Ad rewards are granted only once, after it is confirmed that you watched the ad to the end.

3. Firebase

  • Analytics (analytics and diagnostics, optional): Turned on only if you consented to ‘Analytics and diagnostics’ in section 1. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization signals are always denied, and Android Advertising ID collection is turned off. If you withdraw consent, log out or delete your account, we stop sending data and remove the link to your player ID.
  • Remote Config: Fetches operational settings such as maintenance, required updates and difficulty. An app-scoped install identifier and the app and operating system versions may be processed.
  • Cloud Messaging (notifications, optional): If you turn on Game notifications as described under ‘Notifications’ in section 1, a notification token is created and registered on the game server.

4. In-app purchases

Payments for Gems, bundles, monthly passes, Growth Passes, costumes, Remove Ads and 2x Speed, and auto-renewing subscriptions (Membership and Plush Knights Season Pass) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and each store's policies apply to payment, taxes and refunds. The Developer processes the purchase information in section 1 to verify payments and deliver products.

When you use Settings > Account · Log out · Delete account > Delete Account (confirmed twice), the Game asks the game server to delete your account and the data it holds, and only after the server succeeds does it delete the save data, login information and consent records on your device. Deleting your account does not stop a store subscription, so please cancel it in Google Play or the App Store first. If you cannot use the app, use the email request method in Account Deletion. So that purchases from a deleted account are not delivered again, the minimum transaction records required by law and audit logs with personal details removed may remain. Information processed by Google, Apple and Firebase is kept under each company's policies.

Information whose retention period has ended, or whose deletion you have requested, is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and we do not print personal information or keep it on paper.

6. Processors and international transfers

The following services process information to run the Game. Because the servers are located outside the Republic of Korea, information is transferred abroad. Transfers take place over the network while you use the app (for contact emails, when you send an email).

  • Railway Corporation (USA · contact: Railway Privacy Policy): Hosting and database for the game server (GameStack) operated by the Developer. Stores accounts, cloud saves, server currency, rankings, chat, friends, 1:1 messages and Alliances, public profiles, reports, real-time battle records, notification registrations and promo notification consent records, and purchase, subscription and coupon records. Retention: until you delete your account (the legally required records in section 5 for those periods).
  • Google LLC (USA and other countries · Google Privacy Policy): AdMob (rewarded ads), Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging), Google Play billing, Google sign-in, Play Integrity.
  • Apple Inc. (USA and other countries · Apple Privacy Policy): App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, Apple Push Notification service, App Attest.
  • RevBell (developer Jaemin Song · contact: RevBell Privacy Policy): Receives and stores emails sent to our contact address pungpungstudi@hello-support.com so the Developer can read and reply to them. Processes the contact email information in section 1. Retention: 1 year from receipt (attached images 90 days).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare data centers in the USA and other countries · contact: Cloudflare Privacy Policy): Email receiving, sending and storage service used by RevBell. Receives contact email information when you send an email and stores it for RevBell's retention period.

If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you can stop using the Game and request account deletion. In that case, you will not be able to use the Game, including its online features. Contact emails are not transferred abroad unless you send them, and not sending them does not limit your use of the Game.

7. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of the processing of your information. In the Game, you can do the following yourself.

  • Change your name and Intro, remove friends, block and unblock (Chat > Blocked), and report
  • Turn off Game notifications, promo notifications and promo notifications at night separately in Settings > Notifications (you can also turn them off in your device settings)
  • Turn off Send analytics & error data in Settings
  • Where ad privacy choices are required, change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices
  • Settings > Account · Log out · Delete account > Delete Account

For other requests, please write to the contact address below. To verify your identity, please include your guild leader name and support code (Settings > Account · Log out · Delete account > Copy Support Code).

8. Children

The Game is intended for players 13 and older. Players under 14 cannot use chat or 1:1 messages and cannot consent to analytics and diagnostics or to promo notifications. We ask for notification permission only when you turn on Game notifications. Parents or guardians can request deletion of their child's account and data at our contact address.

9. Security

Communication with the server is encrypted with HTTPS, secret keys for server administration are not included in the app, and access to account data is limited to what is necessary. Payments, Gems and ad rewards are applied only after the server confirms them.

10. Promotional information

  • Promo notifications, such as news about events, products and sales, are sent only to players 14 or older who consented to [Optional] promo notifications. From 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. the next day in the recipient's time zone, they are sent only to players who separately consented to notifications at night.
  • Promo notifications start with “(Ad)” in the title, and the body explains how to stop receiving them (Settings > Notifications).
  • When you consent or withdraw, we immediately tell you the sender (PUNGPUNGSTUDI), the date, and what you consented to or withdrew.
  • Every 2 years from the date you consented, we tell you your current consent status and let you choose whether to keep receiving them.
  • You can use the whole Game without consenting. Game notifications (event start, friend requests, 1:1 messages, offline rewards) are not promo notifications and are turned on separately in Settings.
