Support

Please email us about game errors, payments and restoring purchases, accounts, the community and policies. We accept payment and account inquiries only by email, not on public boards.

  • Email: pungpungstudi@hello-support.com
  • In the Game: Tap Settings > Help > Contact to open an email that includes the app version, platform, time and your support code.
  • We aim to send a first reply within 1 business day for payment inquiries and within 3 business days for other inquiries.

What to include in your inquiry

  • Your guild leader name and support code (Settings > Account · Log out · Delete account > Copy Support Code)
  • App version (‘Version’ at the bottom of Settings), device model, and Android or iOS version
  • When the problem happened, on which screen, and the steps to make it happen again
  • Screenshots without personal information

Please do not send passwords, card numbers, full receipt images, or Google or Apple account information.

Payments and restoring purchases

If you paid but did not receive the product, do not buy it again; restart the app on the latest version. The app checks unfinished purchases again and delivers them. If it still does not arrive, email us your support code, order number (the Google number starting with GPA. or the Apple order ID), product name and payment time. If an inquiry code appeared on the screen, please include it too.

  • Gems and products are recorded on the game server and stay on the same game account. A guest account can only be continued on that device, so please link a Google or Apple account before you delete the app or change devices.
  • Pending payments (such as paying at a convenience store) arrive automatically once you complete the payment.
  • Request refunds from Google Play or Apple. If you get a refund, whatever remains of what you received from that payment is taken back, and your rights to permanent products are turned off.

Cancelling a purchase (Korea)

Within 7 days of purchase, you can request cancellation (withdrawal) of purchase under Korean law for products you have not used. The line “미사용 시 7일 안 청약철회 가능” [Cancellable within 7 days if unused] under the shop cards is this notice.

  • Cancellation is limited for: Gems and summon tickets already used, partly used bundles, permanent products you have started using, monthly passes you have started receiving, Growth Passes with rows already claimed, a Season Pass whose rewards you have started claiming that month, and a Membership whose benefits you have started receiving
  • How: Request a refund from the store where you paid (Google Play or the App Store), or email us with your support code and order number.

Payments by minors

If a minor made a payment without a legal guardian's consent, the minor or the legal guardian can cancel the payment. Request a refund from the store where you paid (Google Play or Apple), and if needed, email us with the support code and order number.

Subscriptions (Membership and Plush Knights Season Pass)

  • A subscription is charged automatically every month until you cancel it.
  • To cancel: on Android, go to Google Play app > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions; on iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. After you cancel, the benefits continue until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Deleting your account in the Game does not stop a store subscription. Please cancel it in the store first.

Save data

Progress is saved on your device and backed up to the game server. You can see the current status in the save row in Settings. If your progress seems to be missing, please contact us with your support code before you delete the app.

Coupons (Android)

In the Android app, enter coupons in Settings > Enter Coupon, and claim the rewards from your Mail. Each coupon can be used once per account and only within the announced period. For coupon problems, email us the code and the time you entered it.

Ad rewards

If you watched an ad to the end but did not get the reward, tell us where the ad was (which screen), the time, and your network status.

Reporting and blocking in chat

You can tap an offensive message, profile or Alliance to report or block it (unblock in Chat > Blocked). If there is urgent harm, please also email us the other player's name and the time. The standards are in the Rules.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the Game at Settings > Account · Log out · Delete account > Delete Account. For what to do when you cannot use the app, and what gets deleted, see Account Deletion.

Drop Rates

The rates for random items such as summons and enhancement are shown in the in-game [Drop Rates] and on the Rates page.
