PUNGPUNGSTUDI (“the developer”) explains here how the mobile game Rumble Alley (com.pungpungstudi.ghostpaws, “the game”) handles personal information. This policy applies to the Rumble Alley version of the app, which replaces the earlier game Ghost Paws in the same app. While you use the earlier Ghost Paws version, the previous version of this policy applies.

Summary: on first launch the game asks you to accept the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy and to confirm that you are 14 or older, and you start with Google, Apple (iPhone) or as a guest. Progress is saved on the device first and backed up to the game server (GameStack). The game does not ask for your name, phone number or address and does not receive payment details. Game-improvement analytics run only if you agree.

2. Advertising

The game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch. It uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may process your IP address (approximate location), device, OS and app information, device or advertising identifiers depending on platform settings and consent, and ad impressions, views and interactions to serve ads, cap frequency, prevent fraud, measure performance and diagnose errors.

  • For every player the game applies the settings for users under the age of consent and child-directed treatment, and requests non-personalized ads.
  • The game does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track players with advertising identifiers.
  • Where required, UMP shows a privacy choices screen, which you can reopen from Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • The developer does not send player IDs to Google. Details: Google Privacy Policy

3. Game-improvement analytics (Firebase Analytics, optional)

Only if you check “[Optional] Help improve the game” on the start screen does the game send the analytics information in section 1 to Google Firebase Analytics. It never sends names, email addresses, player IDs, receipts or text you typed, and advertising identifier collection and ad personalization are turned off. You can play without agreeing and change your choice at any time under Settings → Game analytics. If you withdraw consent or delete your account, sending stops and the waiting data and app instance identifier on the device are reset. Data already sent to Google is deleted according to the retention periods below. About Google Analytics data

4. In-app purchases

Gem purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and each store's policies apply to payment, taxes and refunds. The developer processes the purchase information in section 1 to verify payments and deliver products, and never receives payment details such as card numbers.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal information and withdraw consent. In the game you can use Settings → Game analytics (analytics consent), Ad privacy choices, Hunt alerts and Settings → Account → Delete account; for anything else, email us below. A legal representative or someone you authorize may also make a request. We act without delay and, for correction or deletion requests, tell you the result, or the reason for refusal and how to object, within 10 days of receipt. To request account deletion without the app, see Account and data deletion requests.

8. Children under 14

The game is for players aged 14 and older, and the start screen asks you to confirm your age. If we learn that personal information of a child under 14 was processed, we delete it without delay. A parent or guardian can ask us by email to delete a child's account and information.

9. Security measures

  • Administrative: only the developer can access personal information.
  • Technical: communication with the server is encrypted with HTTPS, server management secrets are never included in the app, access to account data is limited to what is needed, and operator actions are recorded.
  • Physical: the servers run in the infrastructure provider's data centers, and the developer does not print personal information.
