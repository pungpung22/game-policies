Drop rates

As of October 13, 2026

Rumble Alley contains random items (paid loot boxes). The rates below are tables written by the game's own code and match the in-game rate screens. When a rate changes, this page changes with it.

Paid and free diamonds draw at the same rates (free diamonds are spent first). Draws with summon tickets use different rates, and neither the pity nor the pickup applies to them. Summon tickets are no longer given out; only those left can be used.

Pity (guarantee)

  • Rare or better: guaranteed within 10 diamond draws.
  • Hero or better: guaranteed within 30 diamond draws.
  • Legend or better: guaranteed within 100 diamond draws.
  • Myth or better: guaranteed within 300 diamond draws.
  • Ancient or better: guaranteed within 500 diamond draws.
  • Transcendent or better: guaranteed within 1000 diamond draws.
  • Legend or better: from draw 80, the rate rises by 5 percentage points with each draw that misses it.
  • Myth or better: from draw 250, the rate rises by 2 percentage points with each draw that misses it.
  • Ancient or better: from draw 440, the rate rises by 1.6 percentage points with each draw that misses it.
  • Transcendent or better: from draw 900, the rate rises by 1 percentage points with each draw that misses it.

The pity and the rising rates count diamond draws only. When that grade or better comes out, its count starts over.

Pickup

Every 14 days one card of each of the Legend, Myth, Ancient, Transcendent grades becomes the pickup card. When a diamond draw gives that grade, 50% of the time it is the pickup card; the other 50% is shared alike by the rest of that grade. After a non-pickup card of that grade, the next card of that grade is surely the pickup card. The current pickup cards are shown on the in-game draw screen.

Rates per card

A card's rate is its grade's rate divided by the number of cards of that grade (without a pickup). “Base” in the tables means only the 4 starting guns. Buying a new gun with diamonds (Dagger launcher, Sniper rifle) adds that gun's weapon-line cards to the draw, so there are more Advanced, Rare and Hero cards. Dagger launcher line cards come only once the Dagger launcher is bought, Sniper rifle line cards only once the Sniper rifle is bought.

A diamond summon guarantees Hero or better within 30 summons and a Legend within 90. From summon 70, the Legend rate rises by 5 percentage points with each summon that misses it. Summon tickets have no pity. Companions of the same grade come out alike.

Relic keys

Price: 1 key 100 diamonds, 10 keys 1,000 diamonds. One free key every 12 hours (up to 4) and one for each chapter boss cleared for the first time.

One key gives one relic piece. Every candidate relic is equally likely (1 ÷ the number of candidates). At first there are 8 candidates, 12.5% each. Buying guns and getting Rare or better unique cards adds candidates; the in-game relic screen shows the current number and rate.
