Drop rates

As of October 20, 2026

Rumble Alley contains random items (paid loot boxes). The rates below are tables written by the game's own code, and the draw rates match the in-game rate screens. When a rate changes, this page changes with it.

Paid and free gems draw at the same rates (free gems are spent first). Draws with summon tickets use different rates, and neither the pity nor the pickup applies to them. Summon tickets are no longer given out; only those left can be used.

Rates are percentages rounded to four decimal places, as on the in-game rate screens. Because of the rounding, the numbers in a table may add up to slightly more or less than 100%, but the actual rates add up to exactly 100%.

Summon level

The card draw, the companion summon and the Gear Chest each have their own summon level. It rises from Lv1 (the first draw) to Lv10 with that draw's total count (gem draws and any remaining summon ticket draws), and each level's rates are in the tables of each section. If a level is reached during a 10-draw, the new level's rates apply from the next draw. The level never goes down, and the pity, the rising rates and the pickup are the same at every level. Summon ticket draws use their own rates at every level.

Each level reached gives a reward, claimed on the level row in Shop → Draw in the game: gems Lv2 300, Lv3 300, Lv4 600, Lv5 600, Lv6 900, Lv7 900, Lv8 1,500, Lv9 1,500, Lv10 3,000. Lv6 and Lv10 also give one item of a grade, chosen by the player (card draw Lv6 Legend · Lv10 Myth card, companion summon Lv6 Hero · Lv10 Legend companion, Gear Chest Lv6 Legend · Lv10 Myth gear).

Pity (guarantee)

  • Rare or better: guaranteed within 10 gem draws.
  • Hero or better: guaranteed within 30 gem draws.
  • Legend or better: guaranteed within 100 gem draws.
  • Myth or better: guaranteed within 300 gem draws.
  • Ancient or better: guaranteed within 500 gem draws.
  • Transcendent or better: guaranteed within 1000 gem draws.
  • Legend or better: from draw 80, the rate rises by 5 percentage points with each draw that misses it.
  • Myth or better: from draw 250, the rate rises by 2 percentage points with each draw that misses it.
  • Ancient or better: from draw 440, the rate rises by 1.6 percentage points with each draw that misses it.
  • Transcendent or better: from draw 900, the rate rises by 1 percentage points with each draw that misses it.

The pity and the rising rates count gem draws only. When that grade or better comes out, its count starts over.

Pickup

Every 14 days one card of each of the Legend, Myth, Ancient, Transcendent grades becomes the pickup card. When a gem draw gives that grade, 50% of the time it is the pickup card; the other 50% is shared alike by the rest of that grade. After a non-pickup card of that grade, the next card of that grade is surely the pickup card. The current pickup cards are shown on the in-game draw screen.

Rates per card

A card's rate is its grade's rate divided by the number of cards of that grade (without a pickup). “Base” in the tables means only the 4 starting guns. Buying a new gun with gems (Dagger launcher, Sniper rifle) adds that gun's weapon-line cards to the draw, so there are more Advanced, Rare and Hero cards. Dagger launcher line cards come only once the Dagger launcher is bought, Sniper rifle line cards only once the Sniper rifle is bought.

Relic draw

Price: 1 draw 100 gems, 10 draws 1,000 gems (Shop → Draw, Upgrade → Relics in the game). No keys are given for free.

Each draw gives one relic piece. Every candidate relic is equally likely (1 ÷ the number of candidates). At first there are 8 candidates, 12.5% each. Buying guns and getting Rare or better unique cards adds candidates; the in-game relic screen shows the current number and rate.

Each chest gives one piece of gear. The grade rates follow the chest's own summon level with the card draw's table by level (below); the pity and the rising rates are those of the card draw, counted on chest opens only. Within a grade the 16 pieces (4 sets × 4 slots) are equally likely.

Free gear

Gear won for free in chapter battles is not sold for gems, but its rates are published here too. The same 16 pieces as in the Gear Chest come out, and within a grade every piece is equally likely (one piece = the grade's rate ÷ 16).

  • First clear of 1-3: Buddy Gloves (Common), always. This opens gear.
  • After that, the first clear of each chapter stage (except chapter bosses): one piece 35% of the time.
  • First clear of a chapter boss: one gear chest. It holds 3 pieces, each with its own grade roll.

A stage has only one first clear, so each of these comes once per stage. Stage drops and boss chests have no pity, and Myth, Ancient and Transcendent never come out of them.
