This is the previous version, in effect until October 21, 2026. See the current version

Effective and last updated: October 20, 2026

These are the randomized items in Oops Ninja and their odds. They come from the same tables as the in-game Odds window and apply from app version 1.0 (3). We announce any change here and in the game first. Odds are rounded, so a column may differ from 100% by a tiny amount.

Summon tickets come from the shop (paid) and from play (attendance, missions, requests, events and more; free). Every ticket has the same odds wherever it came from (paid and free alike).

1. Gear, skill, relic and pet summons

One summon uses one ticket of that summon; ten summons use ten and are drawn one at a time.

The grade is drawn first; within a grade the kind is drawn with the shares below.

Summon level

Each draw (gear, skill, relic and pet summons, companion summons and falling weapon draws) has a level that rises with its own total draws and raises the odds of higher grades. Each draw uses the level from just before it.

When gear enhancement reaches the levels below it needs a breakthrough. On a failure the attempt's silver and materials are used and the level stays; the next success chance for the same slot and breakthrough rises by 10% (up to 100%). A success resets it.
