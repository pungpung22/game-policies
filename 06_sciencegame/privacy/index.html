1. What the app handles

The app requires no account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or camera.

  • Essential local data: settings, unlocked experiments, selected experiment, purchase entitlements, ad-removal status, and active-play timers are stored on your device.
  • Optional diagnostics: only after you choose “Allow analytics,” Firebase may receive anonymous gameplay events (such as discoveries and active-play checkpoints), app performance, device/app technical information, and crash diagnostics.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob serves contextual/non-personalized ads and may process device, IP, advertising, and ad-interaction information as described by Google. Where required, Google’s consent form is shown before ads are requested.
  • Purchases: Apple or Google processes payments. The app receives product and transaction status needed to unlock a purchase; it does not receive your payment-card details.

2. Why information is used

We use data to run and save the game, restore purchases, prevent excessive ad frequency, diagnose crashes, measure performance, understand feature usage, configure safe gameplay values remotely, and fund the app with advertising.

3. Services and sharing

Data is shared only with service providers needed for these purposes: Google Firebase, Google/AdMob, and the applicable app-store payment provider. We do not sell personal information.

4. Your choices

  • Choose Essential only or Allow analytics at first launch, and change this later in Settings.
  • Open Advertising privacy options in Settings where the regional consent form is available.
  • Purchase Remove Ads to disable automatic interstitial ads.
  • Delete local progress by deleting the app. You may also reset advertising choices through your device and Google controls.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience science sandbox. It does not knowingly collect contact information from children. Advertising is non-personalized.

6. Retention and security

Local data remains until the app is removed or its storage is cleared. Service providers retain optional diagnostics and advertising data under their own policies. We use platform security controls and restrict data collection to what is described above.

7. International processing and updates

Service providers may process information outside your country with their published safeguards. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the official support page.
