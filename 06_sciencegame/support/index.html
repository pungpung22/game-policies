Quick fixes

A purchase is missing

Open Settings in the upper-right corner of the laboratory and choose Restore purchases. Confirm that the device is signed in to the same store account used for the purchase.

An item is still locked

Wait for the store price to load, reconnect to the internet, then reopen the Store. Never purchase the same non-consumable module twice; use Restore purchases instead.

Ads or consent choices

Automatic interstitial ads appear only after at least five minutes of active play and at a natural break. Open Settings → Advertising privacy options to revisit regional choices. Remove Ads permanently disables automatic interstitials.

The laboratory feels stuck

Use Pause/Resume, Undo, or Reset at the top. Restart the app if the simulation still does not respond. Your discovered experiments remain saved locally.

Report an issue

Include the app version, device model, OS version, language, and exact steps. Do not post receipts, account identifiers, or other private information publicly.
