Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 30, 2026

pungpungstudi explains here how Dangerous Her (com.pungpungstudi.dangerousher) handles information.

Summary: No account is required and progress is stored on your device. Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) provide banner, interstitial, optional rewarded ads, and privacy choices. Apple App Store or Google Play processes purchases.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores character and story unlocks, Heart balance, choices, chapter progress, clues, endings, collection items, ad rewards, purchase entitlements, language, and audio settings locally. We do not operate account login or cloud-save servers for this game.

2. Advertising data

Google and its advertising partners may process approximate location derived from IP address, device or app-scoped identifiers, product and advertising interactions, advertising data, crash information, and performance diagnostics to provide, measure, secure, and limit the frequency of ads.

Where required, UMP presents consent choices that can be reopened from the game’s privacy settings. The current game does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission and does not intentionally use IDFA to track users across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

3. In-app purchases

The app store processes payments and refunds; we do not receive full card details. Product IDs, purchase status, and transaction identifiers may be exchanged to deliver and restore purchases. Consumable Hearts are generally not restorable. Non-consumable characters and premium content can be restored with the same store account.

4. Permissions, retention, and deletion

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location permission. Local data remains until app data is cleared or the game is removed. Because there is no developer-operated player account, we have no remote account data to look up or delete.

5. Children and changes

This fictional thriller is intended for adults and is not designed to intentionally collect children’s personal information. We will update this policy and store disclosures if the app’s features, SDKs, or legal obligations materially change.
