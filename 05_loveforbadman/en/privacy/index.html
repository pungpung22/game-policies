Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 7, 2026

pungpungstudi explains here how Dangerous Her (com.pungpungstudi.dangerousher) handles information.

Summary: No account is required and progress is stored on your device. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Google Mobile Ads, and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) provide usage analytics, diagnostics, remote feature settings, ads, and privacy choices. Apple App Store or Google Play processes purchases.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores character and story unlocks, Heart balance, choices, chapter progress, clues, endings, collection items, ad rewards, purchase entitlements, language, and audio settings locally. We do not operate account login or cloud saves. The analytics and diagnostics described below may be processed separately by Google Firebase.

2. Analytics, diagnostics, remote configuration, and advertising

The game uses these Google services:

  • Firebase Analytics to understand app launches, screen and product interactions using app/device information and an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes using stack traces and relevant app, operating-system, device, and diagnostic state.
  • Firebase Remote Config to deliver operational settings, such as ad availability, using app-instance and configuration-request data.
  • Google Mobile Ads and UMP to provide ads, manage frequency and consent choices, measure delivery, provide security, and prevent fraud.

Depending on purpose, these services and Google advertising partners may process approximate location derived from IP address, device or app-scoped identifiers, app, product and advertising interactions, advertising data, crash stack traces, runtime information, and performance diagnostics.

Where required, UMP presents consent choices that can be reopened from the game’s privacy settings. The current game does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission and does not intentionally use IDFA to track users across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell personal information. Firebase is not used to upload story dialogue, player choices, or locally saved game progress.

3. In-app purchases

The app store processes payments and refunds; we do not receive full card details. Product IDs, purchase status, and transaction identifiers may be exchanged to deliver and restore purchases. Consumable Hearts are generally not restorable. Non-consumable characters and premium content can be restored with the same store account.

4. Permissions, retention, and deletion

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location permission. Local data remains until app data is cleared or the game is removed. Firebase and advertising data may be retained and then deleted or aggregated according to Google service settings and retention policies. Because the game has no player account, we cannot look up remote data by a real-name account. You may request deletion or ask a data question using the email below; if the data cannot be identified, we will explain why.

5. Children and changes

This fictional thriller is intended for adults and is not designed to intentionally collect children’s personal information. We will update this policy and store disclosures if the app’s features, SDKs, or legal obligations materially change.
