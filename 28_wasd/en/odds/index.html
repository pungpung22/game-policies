1. Pet, skill and keycap summons

Each summon costs 300 diamonds or one of that summon’s tickets. Batches of 1, 10 and 100 are drawn one at a time.

Summons paid with diamonds and with tickets have the same odds (paid and free alike). Diamonds come from purchases and play; tickets come from events, coupons and the season pass.

The grade is drawn first; within a grade every kind has the same chance.

Summon level

Each category (pets, skills, keycaps) has a summon level that rises with its total summons and raises the odds of higher grades. Each summon uses the level from just before it.

Pity

  • If 19 productions in a row bring nothing rare or better, the 20th is rare or better.
  • If 79 productions in a row bring nothing epic or better, the 80th is epic or better.
  • On a pity roll, the chance of any grade below the guaranteed one moves to that grade; higher grades keep their chances.

Slot

Each of these 10 slots has the same chance, 10%: Housing Armor, Keycap Helm, Switch Core, Stabilizer Boots, Circuit Badge, Ribbon Cape, Contact Gloves, Metal Pauldrons, RGB Module, Plate Shield
