Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 2, 2026

pungpungstudi (“we”) explains how the mobile game WASD Squad (WASD 키우기, com.pungpungstudi.pendoku, the “Game”) handles personal information. From version 2.0.0 this app replaces the earlier game Pengudoku; this policy applies to WASD Squad.

Summary: On first launch the Game creates a guest account automatically and keeps your progress on the device and on our game server (GameStack). The Game never asks you to type a name, email address or phone number. Linking a Google or Apple account and using chat, friends or guilds are optional; when you use them, that information is stored on the server. The Game shows rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and uses Firebase (crash reporting, usage analytics where allowed, remote configuration, push notifications). Payments are handled by the Apple and Google stores. You can delete your account from Settings → Delete account.

1. Information we process and why

Guest account

  • A random installation ID created by the app (not a hardware identifier), a player ID, a nickname (“WASD Player” by default, changeable in Settings) and login session tokens.
  • Purpose: identifying your account, keeping you signed in, saving and restoring progress.

Game progress and cloud save

  • Your whole save: stages and battle records, in-game currencies such as gold and forge tickets, gear, pets, skills, costumes, quest and dungeon records, and game settings.
  • Purpose: the save is written on the device first and backed up to the server, to protect your progress and to prevent abuse.

Server currency and purchases

  • Diamond balance and grant/spend history, and battle progress values used to calculate server-granted play rewards (reached and cleared stage, battle time, defeated enemies and similar).
  • Purchase verification data: store name, product ID, transaction ID, the store’s signed transaction or purchase token, and purchase/refund status.
  • When you buy, your player ID is attached to the store order (Google Play obfuscatedAccountId, Apple appAccountToken).
  • Purpose: verifying payments, delivering items, restoring purchases and handling refunds. We never receive card numbers or other payment details.

Rankings

  • Best stage and battle power scores with your nickname. They are submitted automatically and are visible to other players.

Community (optional)

  • Available only after you accept the community rules and the age confirmation.
  • Plaza, 1:1 and guild chat messages; friend code, friends list and requests; guild name, description and join requests; block list.
  • Reports: the reason, a copy of the reported message and the reported player ID.
  • Your nickname and messages are visible to the people you talk with, your guild members or other players in the plaza.
  • Purpose: providing chat, friends and guilds, handling reports and restricting abuse.

Account linking (optional)

  • On Android you can link a Google account, on iPhone an Apple account, to the same player. The app sends only the sign-in token to the game server.
  • The app does not request your name or email address. A Google sign-in token may contain your Google account email address, which the server may process and keep to confirm the link. Apple sign-in requests no name or email scope.

Notifications, coupons and mail

  • A Firebase Cloud Messaging device token for push notifications, registered with the game server.
  • Coupon codes you enter and your mailbox claim history.

2. Ads and consent

The Game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch; there are no interstitial or banner ads. We use Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and its ad partners may process the following to serve ads, cap frequency, prevent fraud, measure performance and diagnose errors:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID or app-scoped identifiers
  • Device, operating system, app version and network information
  • Ad impressions, completed views, taps, consent status and diagnostics

Where required, UMP shows a privacy choices form, which you can reopen from Settings → Ad privacy. The Game does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the IDFA. We do not send your player ID to AdMob.

3. Firebase

  • Crashlytics (crash reporting): crash logs and diagnostics such as device, OS and app version, to fix errors. Your player ID is attached only when usage analytics below is allowed.
  • Analytics (usage analytics): off at install, and turned on only when the ad consent check (UMP) allows it or where no consent is required. It may process an app instance ID, device and app information, approximate region, app usage events and your player ID.
  • Remote Config: fetches operating settings such as the latest version; an app-scoped installation ID and app/OS version may be processed.
  • Cloud Messaging: creates a push token that is registered with the game server.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases such as diamonds, the monthly pass, costumes and Remove Ads are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, whose policies govern payment, taxes and refunds. We process the purchase information in section 1 to verify payments and deliver items.

5. Retention and deletion

Account information, cloud saves, currency and purchase records and community data are kept while your account exists. Settings → Delete account (confirmed twice) asks the game server to delete your account and the data it owns, and removes the save and sign-in data from your device. Payment and refund records that laws or store policies require us to keep, and the minimum records needed to prevent abuse, may be kept for the required period. Information processed by Google, Apple and Firebase is kept under their own policies.

If a Google or Apple account is linked, deleting from inside the app is not available yet; please use the deletion request on the support page.

6. Service providers and international transfers

The following services process information to run the Game. Their servers may be outside your country, so information may be transferred abroad over the network while you use the app.

  • GameStack (game server, cloud hosting on Railway): account, cloud save, server currency, rankings, chat/friends/guilds, push registration, purchase verification. Kept until the account is deleted.
  • Google LLC (United States and elsewhere): AdMob, Firebase, Google Play billing, Google sign-in. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc. (United States and elsewhere): App Store payments, Sign in with Apple. Apple Privacy Policy

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your information. In the Game you can change your nickname, open ad privacy choices and delete your account; for anything else, contact us below with your nickname and friend code so we can identify the account.

8. Children

Chat, friends and guilds are available only after the community rules’ age confirmation is accepted. Parents and guardians can ask us to delete a child’s account and data.

9. Security

Communication with the server is encrypted with HTTPS, server administration keys are never shipped in the app, and access to account data is limited to what is needed.
