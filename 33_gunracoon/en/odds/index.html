Drop Rates

These are the randomized items in the game and their rates. The numbers are taken directly from the game code (generated November 19, 2026). The rates are the same whether you open with Gems or with a box you got from a product or an ad.

Weapon summon

  • Price: 1 summon 100 Gems, 10 summons 900 Gems. New agents get 15 free summons.
  • The grade of a weapon you get = base grade +0 to +3. The base grade starts at 1 and rises by 1 every 12 newly cleared nights (each time you defeat a region boss), plus the level of the constellation “Summon Master”.
  • The 4 weapon types (Beam, Piercing Rifle, Rapid Shotgun, Blast Launcher) are equally likely (25% each).
  • Pity: if +3 does not appear 39 times in a row, the 40th summon is always +3 (the count restarts when +3 appears).
  • Your summon level rises with your total number of summons; higher levels give better grades.
  • Your very first summon gives the same grade and type as the weapon you are holding (a guided step so you can try merging right away).

Pet Box

  • Price: 1 open 100 Gems, 10 opens 900 Gems. 20 Pet Box Fragments open one box, and Pet Boxes from products open the same way (same rates).
  • The 5 pet types (Firefly, Baby Bat, Lantern Snail, Silver Moth, Frog Rider) are equally likely (20% each). Grades follow the table below; merging two pets of the same type and grade gives the next grade (up to grade 7).

Bulb Box

Weapon options

  • A new weapon gets option lines: 1 line, 2 lines from base grade +2, 3 lines from +4. When you merge weapons, the better line of the two is kept, and a new line is rolled when the line count grows.
  • Each line: one option is chosen with equal chance from the options that fit the weapon type and are not already on another line of that weapon; its rank follows the table below.
  • Reroll: 5 Option Stones or 20 Gems reroll one line (same rates). You can choose between the current line and the new one.
