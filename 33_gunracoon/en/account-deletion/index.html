Account Deletion

Effective: November 19, 2026 (first edition)

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Raccoon Patrol (developer: PUNGPUNGSTUDI). Guest accounts can be deleted too.

Deleting in the Game

  1. Open Settings with the gear button at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap Delete account, check what will be deleted, then tap Yes, delete.
  3. In the final confirmation window, tap Delete account once more.

Only after the game server successfully deletes your account does the Game delete the save data, login information and terms consent records on your device and start over from the beginning. If deletion fails, it is not shown as complete, and you can try again. For an account that signed in with Apple, the link to the Apple account is also removed.

If you cannot use the app (request by email)

Send an email to pungpungstudi@hello-support.com as follows.

  • Subject: [Raccoon Patrol] Account deletion request
  • Body: your agent name, support code (if you know it), how you signed in (Google, Apple or guest), and information that lets us confirm the account (such as part of the order number of a recent payment)

After verifying your identity, we will delete your account and the data below within 30 days and tell you the result by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account, login links, and cloud saves (progress, weapons, pets, bulbs, costumes, etc.)
  • Ranking records and public profile (agent name, portrait)
  • Rights to products you bought (Remove Ads, Permanent 2x Speed, remaining Moonlight Pass period) and ad reward records
  • Save data, login information, terms consent records and regional ad consent records on your device (when you delete in the Game)

What remains

  • Payment and cancellation (withdrawal) records: 5 years as required by law (Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Minimum transaction records, and audit logs with personal details removed, kept so that purchases from a deleted account are not delivered again
  • Server recovery copies (destroyed within 30 days of creation)
  • Information processed by Google and Apple is kept under each company's policies.

For detailed retention periods, see section 4 of the Privacy Policy.

Check before you delete

  • This cannot be undone. A deleted account and its progress cannot be restored.
  • Products you bought (Remove Ads, Permanent 2x Speed, the remaining Moonlight Pass) are also lost, and you cannot receive them again on a new account.
