Rules

Effective: November 19, 2026 (first edition)

These Rules set out the prohibited conduct and community sections of the Terms of Service in detail. You must agree to these Rules before you use chat.

1. Scope

These Rules apply to agent names, global chat, and every place that other players can see, such as rankings.

2. Eligibility

  • Anyone with a server account can view global chat. Only players who link a Google or Apple account can write in it, and only after confirming they are 14 or older and agreeing to these Rules.
  • Anyone who views chat can report and block.

3. What not to do

The following are prohibited everywhere, in agent names and chat.

  • Swearing, harassment or bullying, and insults aimed at other players
  • Hate or discrimination (race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, disability, age and so on)
  • Sexual content, and content that sexualizes minors
  • Threats, or words that encourage violence
  • Spam, advertising or promotion, and links that lead to other services
  • Exposing personal information, yours or anyone else's (real name, address, phone number, email, messenger or social media accounts, etc.)
  • Impersonating staff or other players, and scams
  • Buying or selling accounts, game currencies or items for cash, and sharing accounts
  • Modified apps or automation programs, and knowingly exploiting bugs
  • Repeatedly making false reports

4. Agent names

  • Agent names use Korean letters, English letters and numbers within the set character limit. You can change your name once for free, and after that with Gems.
  • Names that do not follow these Rules may be changed to a default name.

5. Reporting and blocking

  • Tap a chat message to report it or block the person who sent it. Choose one reason: swearing or harassment, hate or discrimination, obscenity, spam or advertising, exposing personal information, or other.
  • When you report, a copy of the reported message is sent to our operations team, and the message is hidden on your screen right away. It is removed for other players only after the operations team reviews it.
  • When you block someone, their chat messages no longer appear on your screen. They still appear in rankings. You can unblock them in Chat > Block list.
  • Reporting and blocking are separate. Reporting someone does not block them.

6. Actions we take

Depending on how serious the violation is and whether it is repeated, we take the following actions.

  • Hiding or deleting messages
  • Muting (you cannot use chat for a set time)
  • Resetting agent names, and excluding you from rankings
  • Suspending the account (for serious violations such as modified apps or payment abuse)

7. Appeals

If you disagree with an action, let us know at pungpungstudi@hello-support.com with your agent name and support code (Settings > Support code > Copy). We will review the action again and tell you the result by email.

8. Record retention

Global chat is not stored on the server. To handle reports, we keep real-time chat evidence for 14 days and handled reports for 365 days after handling. Unhandled reports are kept until they are handled. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
