Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 (first edition) · Same-day update: added global chat and the Rules

PUNGPUNGSTUDI (the “Developer”) explains below how it handles personal information in the mobile game Raccoon Patrol (Android and iPhone pungpungstudi.coralmagic, the “Game”).

Summary: When you first open the Game, you agree to the terms and then start with Google (Android), Apple (iPhone) or as a guest. The Game does not ask you to enter your name, email or phone number. Your progress is saved on your device first and backed up to the game server (GameStack). In rankings and global chat, your agent name and your scores or messages are visible to other players, and only players 14 or older who have linked a Google or Apple account can write in chat. The only ads are rewarded ads (Google AdMob) that you choose to watch, and we do not use personalized ads. We do not use analytics tools. Payments are handled by Google Play and the App Store. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Delete account.

1. Information we process and why

We process the information you need to play the Game in order to perform our agreement with you.

Start-up consent record

  • The version (date) of the Terms of Service you agreed to and of the Privacy Policy you confirmed, and the time you did so. This is kept only on this device and is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Purpose: confirming consent, and asking again when the terms change

Guest account

  • A random install identifier created by the app (not a unique device number), player ID, agent name (a default name at first), and login session token
  • A guest account can only be continued on that device. Before you delete the app or change devices, please link a Google or Apple account in Settings > Link account.
  • Purpose: identifying the account, keeping you logged in, and saving and restoring progress

Account linking (optional)

  • On Android you can start with a Google account, and on iPhone with an Apple account, or link one to a guest account. The app sends the tokens needed to verify your login to the game server. For Sign in with Apple, it also sends a one-time authorization code used to remove the link when you delete your account.
  • The app does not ask you to enter your name or email. However, a Google login token may contain your Google account email address, and the server may keep it to verify the linked account. Sign in with Apple does not request your name or email.

Game progress and cloud saves

  • All save data, including weapon, pet, bulb, constellation, costume and challenge records, game currencies such as Stardust and Gems, and game settings
  • Purpose: saving on your device first and then backing up to the server to protect your progress when you reinstall the app or change devices

Purchases

  • Purchase verification data: store name, product ID, transaction ID or purchase token, purchase and refund status, delivery records, and the period of the Moonlight Pass
  • Purpose: verifying payments, delivering products, preventing duplicate delivery, restoring purchases, and handling refunds. Payment details such as card numbers are processed by Google and Apple; the Game does not receive them.

Ad reward records

  • Ad placement, ad event ID, reward value, and completion and grant times and status
  • Purpose: granting a reward only once and only when it is confirmed that you watched the ad to the end, checking daily limits, and preventing abuse

Rankings and public profile

  • Agent name, the portrait, frame and name tag you chose, weekly patrol, power and world boss damage scores and ranks, and support code
  • Your agent name, portrait, scores and ranks are visible to other players in rankings.
  • Purpose: providing rankings, granting weekly rewards, and verifying your account when you contact us

Global chat (optional)

  • Your confirmation that you are 14 or older and your record of agreeing to the Rules (version and time), made before you use chat
  • Messages you send, the time sent, agent name, player ID, and block list
  • Messages are visible to other players who are viewing chat. Global chat is not stored on the server; to handle reports, real-time chat evidence is kept for a short time (section 4).
  • Purpose: providing chat, filtering banned words, and handling reports and restrictions

Reports

  • The player IDs of the reporter and the reported player, the reason, and a copy of the reported message
  • Purpose: reviewing reports and acting on Rules violations (removing messages, muting, resetting names, suspending use)

Contact email (optional)

  • If you send an email to our contact address: the sender, recipient and reply-to email addresses, subject, body, attached images, email headers, time received and delivery status, and replies sent by the Developer
  • The agent name and support code you give us to verify your identity, and anything else you write in your inquiry (app version and platform, payment time, part of an order number, etc.)
  • Purpose: answering inquiries, handling rights requests such as access or deletion and account deletion requests, and checking payments and errors

2. Ads and consent management

The Game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch; there are no interstitial or banner ads. We use Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and its ad partners may process the following information to serve ads, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, measure performance and diagnose errors.

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device identifiers such as app-scoped identifiers, device, operating system and app version, and network information
  • Interactions such as ad impressions, completed views and taps, consent status, and diagnostic information

Where required (such as the European Economic Area and the UK), UMP shows a privacy choices screen when you first open the Game, and in those regions you can reopen and change it at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. We do not use personalized ads, and we do not use advertising IDs (Android Advertising ID, iPhone IDFA). We do not request Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. The Developer does not send player IDs to AdMob. Ad rewards are granted only once, after the game server confirms that you watched the ad to the end. If you own ‘Remove Ads’, you get the same rewards without ads.

3. In-app purchases

Payments for Gems, the Starter Gift, Remove Ads, Permanent 2x Speed and the Moonlight Pass (30 days, not auto-renewing) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and each store's policies apply to payment, taxes and refunds. The Developer processes the purchase information in section 1 to verify payments and deliver products. The Game has no auto-renewing subscriptions.

When you use Settings > Delete account (confirmed twice), the Game asks the game server to delete your account and the data it holds, and only after the server succeeds does it delete the save data, login information and consent records on your device. If you cannot use the app, use the email request method in Account Deletion. So that purchases from a deleted account are not delivered again, the minimum transaction records required by law and audit logs with personal details removed may remain. Information processed by Google and Apple is kept under each company's policies.

Information whose retention period has ended, or whose deletion you have requested, is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and we do not print personal information or keep it on paper.

5. Processors and international transfers

The following services process information to run the Game. Because the servers are located outside the Republic of Korea, information is transferred abroad. Transfers take place over the network while you use the app (for contact emails, when you send an email).

  • Railway Corporation (USA · contact: Railway Privacy Policy): Hosting and database for the game server (GameStack) operated by the Developer. Stores accounts, cloud saves, rankings, public profiles, global chat delivery and report and block records, and purchase and ad reward records. Retention: until you delete your account (the legally required records in section 4 for those periods).
  • Google LLC (USA and other countries · Google Privacy Policy): AdMob (rewarded ads) and User Messaging Platform, Google Play billing, Google sign-in.
  • Apple Inc. (USA and other countries · Apple Privacy Policy): App Store payments, Sign in with Apple.
  • RevBell (developer Jaemin Song · contact: RevBell Privacy Policy): Receives and stores emails sent to our contact address pungpungstudi@hello-support.com so the Developer can read and reply to them. Processes the contact email information in section 1. Retention: 1 year from receipt (attached images 90 days).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare data centers in the USA and other countries · contact: Cloudflare Privacy Policy): Email receiving, sending and storage service used by RevBell. Receives contact email information when you send an email and stores it for RevBell's retention period.

If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you can stop using the Game and request account deletion. In that case, you will not be able to use the Game, including its online features. Contact emails are not transferred abroad unless you send them, and not sending them does not limit your use of the Game.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of the processing of your information. In the Game, you can do the following yourself.

  • Change your agent name, portrait, frame and name tag, and report, block and unblock in chat (Chat > Block list)
  • Where ad privacy choices are required, change your ad consent in Settings > Ad privacy choices
  • Settings > Delete account

For other requests, please write to the contact address below. To verify your identity, please include your agent name and support code (Settings > Support code > Copy).

7. Children

The Game is intended for players 13 and older. Players under 14 cannot use chat. The Game does not send notifications and does not use analytics tools. Parents or guardians can request deletion of their child's account and data at our contact address.

8. Security

Communication with the server is encrypted with HTTPS, secret keys for server administration are not included in the app, and access to account data is limited to what is necessary. Payments and ad rewards are applied only after the server confirms them.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will post it on this page with its effective date and also announce it in the Game. Changes that are unfavorable to you or important are announced 30 days before they take effect, and other changes 7 days before.
