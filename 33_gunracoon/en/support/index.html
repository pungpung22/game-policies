Support

Please email us about game errors, payments and restoring purchases, accounts, chat, and policies. We accept payment and account inquiries only by email, not on public boards.

  • Email: pungpungstudi@hello-support.com
  • We aim to send a first reply within 1 business day for payment inquiries and within 3 business days for other inquiries.

What to include in your inquiry

  • Your agent name and support code (Settings > Support code > Copy)
  • App version (‘Version’ at the bottom of Settings), device model, and Android or iOS version
  • When the problem happened, on which screen, and the steps to make it happen again
  • Screenshots without personal information

Please do not send passwords, card numbers, full receipt images, or Google or Apple account information.

Payments and restoring purchases

If you paid but did not receive the product, do not buy it again; restart the app on the latest version or tap Settings > Restore purchases. The app checks unfinished purchases again and delivers them. If it still does not arrive, email us your support code, order number (the Google number starting with GPA. or the Apple order ID), product name and payment time.

  • Products you bought are recorded on the game server and stay on the same game account. A guest account can only be continued on that device, so please link a Google or Apple account in Settings > Link account before you delete the app or change devices.
  • Pending payments (such as paying at a convenience store) arrive automatically once you complete the payment.
  • Request refunds from Google Play or Apple. If you get a refund, whatever remains of what you received from that payment is taken back, and your rights to permanent products are turned off.

Cancelling a purchase (Korea)

Within 7 days of purchase, you can request cancellation (withdrawal) of purchase for products you have not used. The line “미사용 시 7일 안 청약철회 가능” [Cancellable within 7 days if unused] in the shop and in the purchase confirmation window is this notice.

  • Cancellation is limited for: Gems already used, partly used bundles (such as when you have opened a box from the Starter Gift), a Moonlight Pass you have started receiving, and permanent products you have started using (Remove Ads, Permanent 2x Speed)
  • How: Request a refund from the store where you paid (Google Play or the App Store), or email us with your support code and order number.

Payments by minors

If a minor made a payment without a legal guardian's consent, the minor or the legal guardian can cancel the payment. Request a refund from the store where you paid (Google Play or Apple), and if needed, email us with the support code and order number.

Moonlight Pass

Each purchase of the Moonlight Pass adds 30 days, and it is not charged automatically. You can see the remaining days in the Moonlight Pass slot in the shop.

Save data

Progress is saved on your device and backed up to the game server. You can see the current status in the save row in Settings. If your progress seems to be missing, please contact us with your support code before you delete the app.

Ad rewards

If you watched an ad to the end but did not get the reward, restart the app after a moment. Rewards confirmed by the server arrive when you restart. If it is still missing, tell us where the ad was (which screen), the time, and the inquiry code shown on the screen.

Reporting and blocking in chat

You can tap an offensive message to report it or block the person who sent it (unblock in Chat > Block list). If there is urgent harm, please also email us the other player's name and the time. The standards are in the Rules.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the Game at Settings > Delete account. For what to do when you cannot use the app, and what gets deleted, see Account Deletion.

Drop Rates

The rates for random items such as summons and upgrades are shown in the Game and on the Rates page.
